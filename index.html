INDICATOR GUIDE

MVRV Z-Score · BGeometrics
Compares Bitcoin's market cap to its "realized cap" — what every coin last sold for on-chain. Below 0 = average holder at a loss; only seen at major cycle bottoms (2015, 2018, 2022). Above 7 has marked cycle tops historically.
Puell Multiple · BGeometrics
Today's miner revenue divided by the 365-day average. Below 0.5 = miners in pain, forced to capitulate — historically aligns with cycle floors. Above 4 = miners overearning, seen near tops.
NUPL · BGeometrics
Net Unrealized Profit/Loss across all holders. Below 0 = most holders underwater (capitulation) — rare and historically a powerful buy signal. Above 0.75 = euphoria, distribution tends to follow.
Price vs Realized Price · CoinGecko proxy
Realized Price = average cost basis of all Bitcoin. When spot drops below it, the average holder is at a loss — only happens at true cycle bottoms. Uses 365-day price average as a free proxy.
Fear & Greed Index · Alternative.me
Composite sentiment (0–100) blending volatility, momentum, social media, dominance, and Google Trends. Sustained Extreme Fear below 20 has historically aligned with bottoms. Sustained Extreme Greed above 80 with tops.
Price vs 200-Week MA · CoinGecko
Bitcoin's long-term floor. Every prior cycle saw the deepest bear market low touch or dip below this level. Currently rising ~$500/week. Within 20% = historically deep value. 3–5x above = historically near peaks.
